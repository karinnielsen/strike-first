# Fighters — roster

Who the nine are. Written by Karin; Astra draws from it. What makes the
drawings usable is in `design/ASSET-BRIEF.md` §8, "Fighters".

Three per dojo. The slug names the files (`<dojo>-<slug>-portrait.png`), so
keep it lowercase, one or two words, and never change it once drawn.

For each fighter:

- **Slug**:
- **Name**: as shown on the card
- **Look**: age, build, hair, skin, what they wear in the dojo's colour
- **Silhouette**: the one thing that tells them apart from the other two at
  18px (hair, headgear, beard, build)
- **Line**: said once, when they are picked
- **Inspired by** (optional): who in the series. The drawing is never that
  actor's likeness
